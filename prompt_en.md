## CONTEXT

Two sports lovers, passionate about volleyball, transformed their desire to play into a greater purpose: creating a project that will help hundreds of young people discover and develop their talents in sports, arts, and culture.  
The Joyce and Teatino Sports, Arts and Cultural Association (J & T) was founded in June 2025 with this mission. Through sponsorships, partnerships, and members, the institution will secure resources to run several sports projects and, in the future, arts and cultural initiatives, promoting sports and education throughout the south of Piauí.

## OBJECTIVE

Build a frontend page with SEO optimization to present the Association, show the different ways to collaborate (as a sponsor or member), and serve as the official business card of the institution.

- **Hero section**: must be eye-catching and attractive, inviting the user to become a partner (sponsor or member).
- **Banner/carousel**: the Hero section must include, or prominently highlight, a banner with an image carousel.
- **Benefits**: the page must clearly explain all the benefits of becoming a partner, whether as a sponsor or member, with SEO-optimized content targeting people who want to learn more about the Association.
- **Pre‑registration**: there must be a pre‑registration section for people interested in becoming partners.
- **Sponsors**: there must be a dedicated area to display sponsors, who will be showcased on the landing page.
- **Footer**: include a footer with quick links and contact methods.
- **Header**: the header must contain a highlighted link such as “Become a Partner”.
- **Recent posts**: include a section with cards showing the most recent posts.
- **UI/UX**: excellent user experience and a well-crafted interface are mandatory requirements.
- **Responsiveness**: the page must be fully _mobile‑friendly_.
- **Themes**: there must be a light theme (as the main one) and a dark theme, strictly following the color scheme defined in `globals.css`.
- **Componentization**: primarily use components from `shadcn` in the latest version.
- **Code quality**: strong focus on good UI/UX and clean code style.
- **Project structure**: generate the complete code, split into different components, organized under a main page.

## CONCEPTS

- **Partners**: can be **members** or **sponsors**. A member is anyone who wants to participate in games, tournaments, and enjoy the benefits offered by the plans. Sponsors contribute through financial sponsorships or by offering benefits to members. Whenever a member uses a benefit, the partner company must validate that member’s registration.

- **How to become a member**: interested people must contact the Association via WhatsApp at **+55 (89) 9 9465‑9400** and send the following information: full name, CPF (Brazilian taxpayer ID), and WhatsApp number. The initial monthly fee will be **R$ 20.00**.

- **Types of sponsors**: sponsors will be classified into three categories: **gold**, **silver**, and **bronze**.

- **Posts/publications**: posts will be managed via **Strapi**, and the landing page must display cards with the most recent publications.

- **Benefits for members**: fixed monthly plan, regular training sessions, access to tournaments, and exclusive benefits.

- **Benefits for sponsors**: guaranteed visibility across all platforms and events, including competitions, social media, and the web platform.
